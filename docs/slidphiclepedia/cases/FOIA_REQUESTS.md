## R1 — USMS Asset Forfeiture (forfeiture amounts, 3 CBP cases)

File: https://www.justice.gov/usms/freedom-information-act (FOIA request form)
Subject: Asset forfeiture records — three named prosecutions

Body:
"Under FOIA, 5 U.S.C. 552, I request all releasable records of assets seized,
forfeited, or listed for forfeiture, including forfeiture amounts, asset
descriptions, and final disbursements, in connection with: (a) the Southern
District of California prosecution of a former CBP officer for opening an
inspection lane to cartel smugglers (USAO-SDCA press release, sentenced);
(b) the Western District of Texas prosecution of a former CBP officer
sentenced March 2026 to 10 years for smuggling/bribery/drug trafficking;
(c) the Southern District of California prosecution of a former CBP officer
sentenced for bribes to ignore smuggling at the Tecate port. Include
AFMLS/Consolidated Asset Tracking System entries. Exclude tax returns and
grand-jury material per statute; release all reasonably segregable portions."

Note: check https://www.forfeiture.gov first — partial data may be public.
