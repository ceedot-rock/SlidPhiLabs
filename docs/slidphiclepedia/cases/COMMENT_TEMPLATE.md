# Pre-release comment request (template — customize per subject, log every send)

Send to: subject or counsel of record + relevant institution press office.
Method: email + certified mail for principals; email for institutions.
Window: 14 days. Publish responses verbatim; note non-responses.

---

Subject: Request for comment — forthcoming report on [CASE-ID topic]

Dear [name/counsel],

We are preparing a report on [one-line topic, e.g. paid facilitation of
drug loads at ports of entry]. Our reporting will state the following
about [you / your office], based on [document: indictment / plea /
sentencing memo / verdict, date, docket]:

1. [Allegation/finding in plain language, with document cite]
2. [Allegation/finding in plain language, with document cite]

We invite your response to each point, in full, by [date, 14 days out].
Any response received by then will be published verbatim alongside the
report. If we receive no response, we will publish that we sought comment
and received none.

We do not publish uncharged allegations against named living persons.
Nothing about you will appear beyond what the cited documents establish,
plus your response.

Contact: [operator contact]
