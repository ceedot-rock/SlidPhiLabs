# LAB50 Steps 14–17 — counsel needs lists (2026-11-10)

Dual license: AGPL-3.0-or-later OR Slid Phi Labs Commercial. See /LICENSE.

These are document checklists, not legal advice. Nothing here is filed, signed,
or sent. Every item below waits on licensed counsel / CPA before action.
Legal v1 site templates (Terms / Privacy / DPA / Refund Policy) are published
as drafts pending that review.

## 14. Waiver review

Bring counsel:
- The exact waiver / release text currently in use (or the draft to be used).
- Who signs it, when in the flow, and what consideration supports it.
- Whether minors, employees, or contractors are in scope.
- State(s) of formation and operation (enforceability varies).
- Ask: scope gaps, severability, whether clickwrap presentation holds up.

## 15. IP assignments

Bring counsel:
- Founder / contributor list with dates of first contribution.
- Current assignment status per person (signed, draft, missing).
- Contractor and advisor agreements — check work-for-hire / assignment clauses.
- Pre-formation IP: anything built before the entity existed needs a written
  assignment into the company.
- Open-source dependencies with copyleft terms touching proprietary code.
- Ask: a single assignment template going forward; cure plan for gaps.

## 16. 83(b) elections — deadline 2026-12-04

Bring CPA + counsel immediately; this deadline does not move:
- Grant dates, vesting schedules, and FMV at grant for each founder grant.
- Confirmation each grant is restricted stock / early-exercise (not options —
  different treatment).
- Mailing plan: 83(b) goes to the IRS service center within 30 days of grant,
  plus copy to the company.
- Ask: who prepares, who mails certified, proof-of-filing retained where.

## 17. Patent — file or abandon

Bring counsel:
- Invention disclosures: what is novel, dates of conception and reduction.
- Any public disclosure dates (demos, posts, papers) — bars may already run.
- Prior-art search results, if any.
- Business call underneath: cost of provisional vs. value of protection vs.
  trade-secret posture.
- Ask: provisional filing recommendation with a dated file-or-abandon decision.
