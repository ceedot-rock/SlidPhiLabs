What Warrant is

Warrant is a signed permission slip for an AI agent. It says exactly what the agent may do — which services it may call, which actions it may take — how many calls it may make, how much money it may spend, and when the permission expires. After the job runs, a receipt is filed: what was done, what it cost, with fingerprints of the request and the result.

Here is why that matters. An agent with your credentials and no boundaries is a liability; an agent with no proof of what it did is an audit nightmare. Warrant solves both at once: the mandate sets the boundaries up front, and the signed receipt records what happened after — it binds the run to the mandate, it does not by itself prove the work was correct. Checking a warrant is free, and anyone can verify the signature without calling home.

Think of it as a hall pass with a spending limit. A human signs it, the agent spends only inside it, and when it expires — or when you revoke it — the authority stops working immediately. Stale permission fails closed: it simply stops being honored.
