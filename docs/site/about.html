Who we are

Slid Phi Labs is a small computation lab in Cherry Hill, New Jersey. Corey Tasz builds it. Inbox: corey@slidphilabs.com. On X: @slidphilabs.

We sell hosted lossless compression at a published price meant to undercut Cloudinary’s first paid plan and AWS S3 egress — not gzip on your own machine. That product is AWARE: $49/month with 200 GB included, then 8¢/GB. The encoder stays here. Numbers are measured. Price table.

The other products are tools that check. CuNi prints one program into many languages, then refuses unless Python, Go, and JavaScript match. Chamber splits a JSON secret across two keys. Agent-Rider signs the agent so you know which one acted. pulsar is the free compressor you download. Closed-source shipping of pulsar needs a written $490/year exception.

Humans pay with Stripe. Agents pay with x402 (USDC) and can use the hosted MCP.
