Who we are

Slid Phi Labs is a small computation lab in Cherry Hill, New Jersey. Corey Tasz builds it. Inbox: corey@slidphilabs.com. On X: @slidphilabs.

The goal is simple to say and hard to keep: ship tools that check. Same result on three languages, or refuse. A JSON secret that will not open without both keys. An agent that is signed, or it is not trusted. Compression numbers you can measure, without pretending the public git tree is the production engine.

We sell access to those tools. CuNi Studio is free. Chamber, Agent-Rider, and AWARE are paid seats. You can try Chamber or AWARE for 24 hours. After that it stops unless you buy. Public libraries (pulsar, blackjack, shards) are GPLv3. Closed-source shipping of those libraries needs a written exception. Hosted seats are access, not a copy of the engine.

Humans pay with Stripe. Agents pay with x402 (USDC) and can use the hosted MCP.
