About

A compression lab. That is the direction.

Slid Phi Labs is a small computation lab in Cherry Hill, New Jersey. Corey Tasz builds it. Inbox: corey@slidphilabs.com. On X: @slidphilabs.

The lead product is PCC: hosted lossless compression. You send a file. We run every pathway we own. You restore every byte later. Dual-licensed: open terms, or a paid grant to ship closed. The bill is on Pricing.

Same plan for live logs (TRUSTREAM). Chamber locks JSON with two keys. Agent-Rider names the agent. Warrant writes the mandate and the receipt. CuNi writes once; Python, Go, and JavaScript must match. pulsar is the free compressor you run on your own machine.

Humans pay with Stripe. Agents pay with x402 (USDC) and can use the hosted MCP.
