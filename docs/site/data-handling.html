Data-Handling Policy — PCC & TRUSTREAM

Slid Phi Labs · DRAFT FOR REVIEW — not published, not in effect

Plain-English version of what happens to the files you send us. If the plain

version and the legal version ever disagree, the plain version wins — tell us

and we'll fix the legal one.

What we store

  • The files you upload, the compressed archives we produce, and **job

records**: byte counts, SHA-256 hashes of input and output, timestamps, and

decode-verification results.

  • TRUSTREAM tiles you send us (log/telemetry data), stored under the same rules.
  • What we don't store: payment card details (Stripe handles those — we never

see them). We don't buy, sell, or trade your data, and we share it with nobody

except as described below.

Encryption

  • In transit: everything moves over HTTPS/TLS. [VERIFY: confirm TLS config on

the API hosts before publishing.]

  • At rest: our commitment is AES-256 (or platform-equivalent) encryption at

rest for customer files and archives. **[FLAG: implementation not yet verified

— confirm before this policy publishes. Do not ship this paragraph until it's true.]**

Who can see your data

  • Corey Tasz, the lab's sole operator — for support, debugging, and keeping

the service running. That's the whole access list.

  • Our infrastructure provider (Fly.io) has underlying host-level access, the

way any hosting provider does. No advertisers, no data brokers, no "partners."

Retention

  • API job outputs: purged 7 days after the job completes. Re-download or re-run.
  • Archives you keep stored with us: persist until you delete them.
  • TRUSTREAM tiles: rolling 30-day retention, then purged. [PROPOSED — Corey decides.]

Deletion

  • On request: email corey@slidphilabs.com from your account email. We delete

your files, archives, and job records within 14 days and confirm by email.

[PROPOSED SLA — Corey decides.]

  • If you stop paying: 30-day grace period to download or delete everything,

then we purge. [PROPOSED — Corey decides.]

  • Deletion means deleted from live systems; backups (if any) age out on their own

cycle within 90 days. [PROPOSED — confirm backup story is real before publishing.]

If something goes wrong

If your data is exposed or lost because of us, we tell you directly and promptly.

No burying it in a status page footnote.

What we're not

We're a one-person lab. We have not completed SOC 2, ISO 27001, or any

third-party security audit. This page describes what we actually do — not a

certification we don't have. If you need a signed DPA or a custom security

review, email corey@slidphilabs.com and we'll talk.

Questions about this policy: corey@slidphilabs.com
