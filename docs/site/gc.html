AWARE

A hosted lossless compressor. You send bytes, you get smaller bytes back, you can restore them. You are buying a seat to run it — not a copy of the engine.

The product name is AWARE. The engine behind the seat is Combined GC. We publish measurements. We do not put the encoder in npm or on the public default branch.

What you get: day, month, or year access, or metered jobs through Suite (6.9 GB / 3 hours unpaid, then about 5¢/GB). A 24-hour try is on /box.

Who it is for: people who need lossless compress on a hosted API, and who care that the claim is measured. Not for anyone who wants the source in a tarball.

pulsar is a separate public GPLv3 compressor you can build yourself. It beats gzip on Silesia and loses to xz. It is not Combined GC. Do not buy AWARE because you saw a pulsar table, and do not buy pulsar numbers expecting the private engine.
