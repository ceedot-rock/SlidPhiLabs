Why the price. Cloudinary Plus is $99/month for 225 credits. AWS S3 first-tier internet egress is about 9¢/GB and does not compress anything. TinyPNG is 0.9¢ per image — right for thumbnails, the wrong product for archives. We publish the table on /compare. We are not cheaper than gzip on your laptop, and we are not a $0 CDN.

Files and a live pipe, one plan. Ordinary archives go through as a whole file. Agent logs and SIEM can go through as a 4 KiB stream: zeros collapse, already-random chunks stay the same size. That stream is TRUSTREAM. Same SKU. You are not billed twice.

Who it is for. Teams that want lossless compression as a service — backups, datasets, logs — without running the encoder themselves and without paying media-platform prices. Who it is not for: anyone who needs the source in a tarball. The encoder stays in the lab. pulsar is the free compressor you download and build if you want code in your hands.
