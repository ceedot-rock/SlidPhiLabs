What it is

AwLPay is a payment product with no allowlist. It accepts any token on any chain — if the token has a verifiable price, it pays. You don't need to ask whether your chain or your coin is supported. If it has value, it works.

Paying with crypto today means navigating a maze: is this chain supported, is this token accepted, which bridge, what rate, what fee. AwLPay collapses that to a single question — does it have a verifiable price? — and handles the rest. Its converter turns anything into anything whenever a conversion path exists and the amount survives the fees.

And when the math doesn't work, it refuses the payment instead of giving you a bad rate. No silent slippage, no mystery haircut — a clean refusal beats a quietly unfair deal.

Right now AwLPay is an API, not a web app. The quote route is free; the execute route answers with a 402 and the payment terms. There is no signup page and no dashboard yet — the docs have the honest picture.

Design

Starts pragmatic — an attested relayer with Chamber signatures — and is designed to move toward trust-minimized light-client proofs. Signed agent identity and bounded spend boxes come from Rider; CuNi is the exact law on every conversion path.
