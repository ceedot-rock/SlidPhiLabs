What it is

A payment product with no allowlist. If the token has a verifiable price, it pays. The ConverterRouter converts anything to anything when a path exists and the amount survives fees — otherwise it refuses instead of guessing.

Tiers

  • Free — 1.0% + $0.25 per payment.
  • Pro $39/mo — 0% platform fee under the cap (cap: $30k volume or 500 txs/mo); overage falls back to Free pricing.
  • L33t $799/mo — unlimited with a fair-use compute guard.

Design

Starts pragmatic — an attested relayer with Chamber signatures — and is designed to move toward trust-minimized light-client proofs. Signed agent identity and bounded spend boxes come from Rider; CuNi is the exact law on every conversion path.
