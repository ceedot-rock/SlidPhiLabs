Stream · residual pipe

TRUSTREAM

A live 4 KiB residual pipe. You point it at agent logs, SIEM, or telemetry. Zeros collapse. Incompressible chunks do not grow. It is not a general file compressor.

AWARE is the hosted archive compressor — files in, files out, a seat. TRUSTREAM is the stream. zstd stays the fast general codec. pulsar is the public GPLv3 demo. Do not buy AWARE because you needed a log pipe, and do not expect TRUSTREAM to beat zstd on text.

What it is for: agent logs, SIEM, zero-heavy traces. Measured on incompressible: TRUSTREAM 1.0000 · zstd 1.0001 · gzip 1.0002. Zeros × 10k go to 8 bytes. Not claimed: beats zstd on text.

No separate SKU yet. It runs with an AWARE seat or a Suite job. Source stays in the lab.
