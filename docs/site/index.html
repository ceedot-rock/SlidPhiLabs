How a purchase works

  1. 1 · Pick the jobA call is one action, such as check or compress. A seat is a month or a year of a product, such as Chamber or Rider.
  2. 2 · See the priceCalls show a dollar amount or a meter. Seats show the checkout price. Nothing on this page is a surprise fee.
  3. 3 · Pay the door that matchesHumans use /pay. Agents get HTTP 402 and pay with the X-PAYMENT header. The Studio at cuni-studio.fly.dev stays free.
