01

Where the money goes — and where it stops.

Four enterprise costs. Four technologies that remove them. Every number below is measured in public.

Enterprise pain points, the Slid Phi Labs solution, and the direct financial impact
Enterprise pain pointThe Slid Phi Labs solutionDirect financial impact
Agents that burn 96.8% more context than they need PERsiST — the compressed heart. Our agents fold all 354 shards of carried knowledge (psi rules + AgentBibleCodec chapters + task payloads) into a single LZMA-9 + PCC-CAE compressed archive, inflate only the ~3% slice each task actually needs, and verify every read cryptographically. Measured on our own fleet: 61,112 → 1,976 tokens per agent turn (96.8% reduction), with `354/354` shards SHA-256-verified and `11/11` Ed25519 receipts. AI context costs money on every turn, on every seat, forever. A 96.8% reduction at typical frontier-model rates ($3/1M) is roughly $37/month per seat at just 7 turns/day — before counting fewer hallucinated edits and fewer pushes you have to revert.
Volatile data-center overhead Compression that holds up to measurement. Our TNSSRC engine compressed the 211,938,580-byte Silesia reference corpus to 43,724,575 bytes — ~11% smaller than xz-6 (49,233,340 B) and ~17.5% smaller than zstd-19 (53,024,573 B) on the same corpus — a result independently verified on the Large Text Compression Benchmark. Our PCC codec is the ratio leader for working data: 22.7:1 on text, 27:1 on JSON, 500:1 on code (lab-measured). Storage and transfer costs scale with footprint. A pipeline that lands 11–17.5% smaller than the standard reference pipelines shrinks every downstream line item attached to those bytes — primary storage, replication, backup, and egress — by the same proportion, on every cycle, forever.
Agents that spend without authority Warrant: mandates enforced before payment. Every payment an agent attempts is checked against a human-set mandate first. In-bounds spend executes; out-of-bounds spend is blocked, not logged — the loss never happens. The enforcement core is verified across two independent implementations (17/17 tests in TypeScript, 8/8 in the Python port). Unauthorized spend that never executes is a loss line that goes to zero — not detected, not disputed, not written off: prevented. Finance keeps a hard ceiling on autonomous spend without slowing the work that stays inside it.
Agentic inefficiency — no record that stands up Agent-Rider + CuNi: identity and evidence for agent work. Agent-Rider gives every agent a signed identity on a live hire rail (a production MCP server, 66 tools) where agents post, claim, and settle work. CuNi issues a tamper-evident receipt for every cross-agent action, with receipt batches Merkle-anchored on-chain — a trail a third party can verify independently, offline, without trusting us. Disputes get closed by verification instead of negotiation. Audit preparation stops being a reconstruction project: the evidence already exists, is already ordered, and already proves itself. In our end-to-end scenario — run twice against the live rail — 12/12 receipts verified offline in each run and spend reconciled exactly: that's what "the books balance themselves" looks like as an engineering fact.

Compression comparisons are named-corpus measurements: Silesia reference corpus (211,938,580 bytes), TNSSRC result verified on the Large Text Compression Benchmark; PCC ratios are lab-measured on the lab's corpora. The verified TNSSRC mark is the headline; our current public build encodes larger — the mark, the build, and the gap between them are all published in our benchmark notes, because a number we can't show our work for doesn't go on this page. 03

What it is. What it isn't.

What it is: A computation and AI company that sells certainty. We build compression engines with independently verified results, and the accountability layer for autonomous agents — identity, mandates, and receipts — then we publish our measurements so customers and investors never have to take our word for anything.

What it isn't: Not a consultancy — we ship products, not hours. Not a model lab — we don't train foundation models or sell tokens; we build the infrastructure that makes other people's agents safe to deploy and cheap to run. Not a demo shop — everything named on this site is live, measured, or explicitly labelled research-stage.
