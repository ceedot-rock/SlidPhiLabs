Refund Policy

Slid Phi Labs · DRAFT FOR REVIEW — not published, not in effect

Simple rules, no fine-print traps. All numbers below are proposals — Corey decides.

The short version

  • Monthly and annual plans: full refund within 14 days of the charge if

you've used less than 10% of the plan's quota. (Mirrors the Founding 20's

14-day money-back promise.)

  • Usage billing (8¢/GB and overages): non-refundable once the compute is

consumed — the machines already ran.

  • Day passes ($9): non-refundable once your first job runs; refundable if

you never run anything.

  • Founding 20 early-access plans: 14-day money-back as promised at purchase,

no quota math — our word is our word.

How to get one

Email corey@slidphilabs.com from your account email with "refund" in the

subject. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 10

business days. [PROPOSED timeline — Corey decides.]

What we won't do

  • No refund for "the ratio wasn't what I hoped." Compression results vary by

data; the free 2 GB tier exists so you can check your own files first.

  • No refunds for accounts suspended for abuse or illegal use.
  • Chargebacks without contacting us first get the account closed. Talk to us —

we're one person and we'd rather fix it.

If we mess up

If the service fails on our side (verified job failures, extended outage on a

paid tier), we credit or refund the affected period without you having to argue

for it. [COMMITMENT — Corey confirms the trigger definition before publishing.]
