PCC vs the usual bill

PCC is hosted lossless compression: send a file, get a smaller one, restore every byte. $49/month includes 200 GB, then 8¢/GB. Cloudinary Plus starts at $99. AWS S3 charges about 9¢/GB to send the file out uncompressed.

We are not cheaper than gzip on your own machine. We are not cheaper than a CDN that charges $0 to send bytes (Cloudflare R2). Those are not hosted lossless compressors.

We are cheaper than paying Cloudinary $99 to start, cheaper than paying AWS ~9¢/GB to ship uncompressed files, and cheaper per gigabyte than TinyPNG once the files are large. The lab publishes the meter. Source is dual-licensed. Hosted PCC is the grant that runs every own pathway on our machines.
