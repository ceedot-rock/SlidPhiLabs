Pay by the gigabyte

Suite

You have a lot of files to compress and you do not want to buy a yearly seat. Suite is a meter. The first 100 gigabytes are free. After that you pay about 5 cents a gigabyte, or about 4 cents if the job is bulk. You are buying work done, not a license to the tools.

It is

A usage meter for encode/decode jobs. 100 GB free, then ~5¢ / ~4¢. Separate from the yearly products.

It is not

Chamber. Not TRU8 Year. Not Gate. Not the game. Buying Suite does not unlock those seats.

When to use this vs a seat

If you want to lock secrets, keep a compressor on all year, or play the game, buy a product seat or Lab Pass. If you have a pile of gigabytes to run through once, use Suite.
