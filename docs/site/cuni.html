What you get: a browser playground where you write a program, emit it, and pick the language in a list. Hit exactness and the compiler emit+runs the catalog. Native seats today: Python, Go, JavaScript, TypeScript, C, C++, and Rust. Other catalog ids still emit+run (Python lowering until that seat is native). If stdout would not match, it says no and does not publish.

Who it is for: people who are tired of a script that works in one language and lies in another. Teams that need one rule, printed everywhere — pricing, citizenship, license, meter.

Studio is free. Dual license: AGPL-3.0-or-later, or SKU cuni-exception $490 / year to ship CuNi in a closed product. Protocol: cuni-protocol.json.
