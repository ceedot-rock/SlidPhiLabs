FAQ

Can I use it without an account? Yes. Lookup tools on https://www.slidphilabs.com/mcp need no token. Fair use is 60 requests/hour and 500/day per IP. An agent key raises that to 200/hour and 2 000/day and counts against the account, not the shared office IP.

Does this dump the engine? No. Catalogs are public law: names, prices, surfaces, published measures, license posture. The engine and Chamber bytes stay behind a seat.

How do I buy after a lookup? Humans: /pay?sku=… on Stripe. Agents: POST /api/x402-products with the SKU, then pay the 402. Or call spl_catalog then follow the rail the tool returns.
