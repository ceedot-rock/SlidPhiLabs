Policy · privacy

Your data stays yours.

What we collect, what we never see, and how to get deleted. Effective 2026-11-10, v1.

What we collect

  • To operate: request payloads while processing, account identifiers where you sign in, and a money-only sales ledger (SKU, amounts, order id — never email).
  • To debug: minimal server logs, rotated, never full third-party content.

What we never see

  • Card and bank details — those go to Stripe, never our servers.
  • Private keys and wallet secrets — signing happens on your side.

What we never do

No sale of personal data. No advertising profiles. Sub-processors are limited to hosting, payments, and the model rails a given product names.

Deletion

Write to corey@slidphilabs.com for a copy or deletion of your records. Sales-ledger lines required for tax records are kept as the law requires; everything else goes.
