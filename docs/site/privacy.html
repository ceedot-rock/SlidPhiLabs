Privacy Policy

Slid Phi Labs · DRAFT FOR REVIEW — not published, not in effect

We collect as little as possible, we don't sell anything, and you can ask us to

delete what we have.

What we collect

  • Account info: your email address. That's the account.
  • Payment info: processed by Stripe. We see that you paid and which plan —

we never see or store your card number.

  • Usage data: how much you compress, API calls, job metadata (byte counts,

hashes, timestamps). We need this to run the meter and keep the service working.

  • Support emails: what you send to corey@slidphilabs.com, kept so we can

actually help you.

What we don't do

  • No selling your data. No sharing it with advertisers, brokers, or "partners."
  • No ad trackers on the site. [COMMITMENT — verify no third-party analytics with

data-sharing is embedded before publishing.]

  • We don't read your files beyond what's needed to compress, verify, and debug

them. See the data-handling policy for the full file story.

Cookies

The site uses the minimum cookies needed to keep you logged in and remember

basic preferences. No cross-site tracking cookies. [VERIFY against actual site

implementation before publishing.]

Your rights

  • See it: ask what we hold about you: corey@slidphilabs.com.
  • Delete it: ask and we delete your account data per the data-handling

policy (14-day completion, proposed).

  • Leave: cancel anytime; the data-handling policy covers what happens next.

How long we keep it

Account and billing records: as long as the account is active, plus what's

legally required for tax records. Usage logs: 12 months, then aggregated or

purged. [PROPOSED — Corey decides.]

Changes

Same rule as the terms: material changes get posted 14 days before taking effect.
