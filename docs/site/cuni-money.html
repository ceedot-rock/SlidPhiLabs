Money: the same accounting on every seat, or refuse

Money is where a rounding difference becomes a real loss. CuNi states the money law once — in dec fixed-point and time epochs, never floats — and proves it byte-identical on the money seats before anyone relies on it.

Laws already in production use

  • spend-control (amount <= cap) — the law behind Warrant spend gating. See Warrant.
  • rider-fee — the fee schedule an agent's seat settles against. See Rider.
  • suite-meter — metering for the lab's own suite of tools.
  • catalog-plans — plan and pricing arithmetic, in the same money types.

The attenuation rule in a Warrant chain — budgets only shrink down the chain — is exactly the kind of monotonic invariant CuNi laws are built to express.

What is next, honestly labeled roadmap

  • Warrant-chain accounting law: chain verification + ledger summation + total ≤ root invariant, gated on the money seats. Milestone: a 3-level chain, 1,000-call ledger, total ≤ root proven byte-identical on py/rs/go/java/sql/js; a tampered ledger refused on all seats.
  • x402 settlement reconciliation: replay the live per-call ledger through the money laws and prove the settled totals match the law's accounting on every seat. Milestone: one week of mainnet x402 calls replayed through rider-fee + suite-meter, totals matching to the cent on all money seats.

Neither milestone is claimed live today. When one lands, this page will say so, with the receipt.
