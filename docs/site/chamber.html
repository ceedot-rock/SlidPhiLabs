What you do with it

You call cloak on the JSON. Chamber splits the message key into two independent shares and encrypts the payload with AES-256-GCM. You can store or ship the sealed blob. To get the original back you call open with both shares and a license that has not expired or been edited.

If someone tampers with the license file, Chamber shuts down. When the 24-hour try or the paid seat ends, it refuses every operational call until you buy again.
