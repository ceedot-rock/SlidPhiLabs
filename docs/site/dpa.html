Policy · DPA

Your data, processed narrowly.

For customers on API and product tiers: what we process, why, and your rights. Effective 2026-11-10, v1.

Scope

We process customer data only to provide the contracted service: run the requested endpoint, settle the meter, debug failures, and meet legal duties. Nothing else.

No training on your data

Payloads sent to our endpoints are not used to train models. Evaluation aggregates, if any, are anonymized beyond re-identification before use.

Sub-processors

Hosting (Fly.io), payments (Stripe), and the model rails each product names. We sign our own DPAs upstream and will list changes here before they take effect.

Security and breach

Transport encryption throughout, least-privilege access, rotated secrets. If a breach exposes customer data we notify affected customers without undue delay with what happened, what was affected, and what we did.

Return and deletion

On termination, customer content is deleted on request within 30 days except records the law requires us to keep (money lines, security logs), which are minimized and access-logged.
