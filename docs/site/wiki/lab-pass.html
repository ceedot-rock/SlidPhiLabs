What it is

Lab Pass is the yearly bundle: a Chamber license year, a PCC license year, and one psi seat. It is the lab membership card.

How it works

  1. Buy the pass once.
  2. Seats light up on Chamber, PCC, and psi.
  3. Renew once a year.

What you receive

You receive a Chamber year, a PCC year, and a psi seat, billed once at $890. Commercial use is covered; enterprise terms are on request.

Price

$890 a year. The three parts bought separately cost more. Read /pricing for the current numbers.

Works with

Chamber keeps the memory. PCC packs the files. psi runs the bench.
