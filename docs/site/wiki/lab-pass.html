What it is

Lab Pass is the yearly bundle: a Chamber license year, a Rider Solo year, a PSI Meter seat year, and a Warrant add-on year. It is the lab membership card. The parts bought separately cost $650; the pass is 24% off. There is no monthly version.

How it works

What you receive
