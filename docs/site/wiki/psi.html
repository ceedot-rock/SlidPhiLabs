What it is

psi is the licensed runtime tree: a boot script, a doctor gate that reports PSI-GREEN, an append-only observatory log of boots and fixes, a retrieval scorer over past notes, preflight gates, thrift rites, and a savings meter against a conservative price book.

How it works

  1. Take a license.
  2. Run the boot.
  3. The doctor checks the wiring.
  4. Work; every fix lands in the observatory.
  5. Retrieval surfaces past answers before new spend.

What you receive

You receive the licensed runtime tree: boot, doctor, retrieval, preflight gates, thrift rites, the observatory log format, and the savings meter. Lab Pass includes a PSI Meter seat.

Price

PSI Meter is $25 a seat a month, $250 a seat a year, including 250,000 logged calls a month, then $2 per additional 100,000 calls, with a customer-set monthly cap and an alert at 80% of the cap. Free: 1 seat, 10,000 calls a month, 7-day retention. PSI Embed, the commercial code license, is the unified grant: $490 a year for one product, $190 for the first 10 grants, $390 each for 3 or more products. Written, human-fulfilled, one product and one year, renewal at the then-current price. The psi-month and psi-year SKUs are retired. Lab Pass ($495 a year) includes a PSI Meter seat. Teams seat a crew with psi Org. Read /pricing for the current numbers.
