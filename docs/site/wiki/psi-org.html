What it is

psi Org is the team lane for psi: licensed seats for a crew, billed together. It carries the same runtime every seat runs: boot, doctor gate, observatory log, retrieval, and savings meter.

How it works

  1. Buy one seat per teammate.
  2. Each seat boots its own runtime.
  3. The crew shares notes through the observatory habit.

What you receive

You receive one licensed psi seat per teammate on a single bill, with the full runtime tree each seat runs.

Price

Per-seat pricing is on /pricing. Commercial only; enterprise terms are on request.

psi is the seat itself. Lab Pass is the individual bundle.
