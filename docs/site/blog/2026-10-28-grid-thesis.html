Intelligence is becoming infrastructure. We build the rails.

A century ago electricity stopped being a marvel and became a metered utility. The fights that followed — who generates, who prices, who writes the code, what gets built on top — are the fights AI is walking into now.

The signs are measurable, not vibes. Millions of AI agents already transact on public networks. Inference — serving answers, not training models — is now roughly two-thirds of all AI compute. Regulators on three continents are writing the incorporation papers of the machine economy as we speak.

Our thesis, in one line: the money in the next decade goes to whoever owns the rails — identity, receipts, and metering. Models commoditize. Rails compound. Here's how our products map:

  • Rider is identity. Every agent gets a signed name strangers can check — ES256 JWT, verified locally via JWKS, no round trip. agentrider.slidphilabs.com
  • Warrant is authorization. A written mandate saying what an agent may do — the permission slip the future's liability law will ask for.
  • PSI is accounting. Every model call tagged with what it cost — tokens, dollars, carbon, time. You can't cut what you won't face. psi.slidphilabs.com
  • ACP is settlement. Offer to invoice to receipt — the machine economy's cash register. acp.slidphilabs.com
  • CuNi is verification. Same program, same output, or refuse — exactness you check instead of trust. cuni.slidphilabs.com
  • PCC is margin. Hosted lossless compression that shrinks what inference must serve — every byte back, proven by decode plus SHA-256. pcc.slidphilabs.com

The pattern across all six: proof over promise. Signed credentials, filed receipts, metered spend, decoded bytes. The machine economy will run on evidence, and evidence is what we sell.
