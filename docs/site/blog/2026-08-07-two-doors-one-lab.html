The product story (public)

Under both doors sit the same two modules:

  • Compression — SPL Codec (web + suite) and CDDG:Split as a process product name you can buy

Agent process (Agentic Minded)

Agents get machine rails. They do not get a dump of the vault. Same IP Guard as humans — different wire format.
