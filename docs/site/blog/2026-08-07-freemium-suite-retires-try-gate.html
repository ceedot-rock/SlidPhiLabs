Try Gate retired: free first 1 GB, then usage

Published 2026-08-07 · Same day as freemium suite launch

We dropped the $9 Try Gate chip-in. Evaluation is now the same path as production suite jobs: feel the platform free, then pay only for usage over a clear cap.

IP Guard: Pricing and public rails only. Private process and engines stay in the lab.

Why we switched

Two parallel “try” stories confused people: a paid seat and a metered suite. The suite freemium model is simpler and more honest — showcase power on real bytes, then charge for volume at rates far under typical cloud egress.

What changed (timeline)

  • 2026-08-07 Freemium suite pricing shipped. First 1 GiB free per job. Above that: ~1.5¢/GB (0.8¢/GB after 50 GB over free). Min paid charge $0.15.
  • 2026-08-07 Try Gate retired from public product. Nav, pricing cards, agent catalog, and discovery no longer push $9 chip-in. /try 308 → /pps.
  • Still available License packages unchanged. CDDG:Split, ZRW tiers, support/consulting/sponsor — fixed Stripe SKUs for full product access.
  • Legacy Old Try Gate payments can still verify on Access if someone already paid. New buyers should not use that path.

How to evaluate now

  1. Free web — /web for instant compress/decompress demos.
  2. Free suite — /pps, set size ≤ 1 GB → $0 → continue free → submit.
  3. Usage — over 1 GB, quote shows cents/GB; pay Stripe (humans) or x402 (agents).
  4. Full packages — CDDG / ZRW when you want a licensed process product, not just a job.

Pricing vs competition (usage)

Typical cloud egress is often on the order of ~$0.05–$0.09/GB. Our paid suite rate is about 1.5¢/GB after free — intentionally far under that class, so ROI is obvious when compression actually shrinks transfer or storage tax.

Examples (auto · compress): 1 GB = $0 · 9 GB ≈ $0.15 · 50 GB ≈ $0.74.

Agents

GET /api/agent now points starter traffic at /pps and documents freemium suite pricing. POST /api/x402-suite accepts free jobs under 1 GB without payment; over free, x402 as before. Standing license SKUs remain on the catalog — try-gate is not listed.
