pulsar

The open lossless compressor from Slid Phi Labs. You can build it. It is not Combined GC and it is not the hosted AWARE seat.

Official Silesia 12 files, encoded individually: 55,745,438 bytes of 211,938,580. DECODE_OK 12/12. Beats gzip-9 (67,631,990). Loses to bzip2-9 (54,506,769) and xz-6 (~49.4M). Not a rank claim against paq8px, cmix, or zpaq.

Release: github.com/ceedot-rock/pulsar-best v2.5.0. Linux x86_64 binary is on that page. Source is GPL-3.0-or-later.

If you need a hosted seat with the private engine, that product is AWARE. Do not buy AWARE because you saw this table, and do not expect pulsar to match the private engine.
