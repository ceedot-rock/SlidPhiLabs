Short version: we run a compression and tooling service. You use it lawfully,

you pay for what you use, and we do our best to keep it running. The long

version is below, in plain language.

1. Who this is between

You and Corey Tasz, doing business as Slid Phi Labs, Cherry Hill, New Jersey.

[FLAG: lab is not yet incorporated — update this section if/when it is.]

2. What you get

  • PCC: hosted lossless compression. You send files; we return the smallest

verified copy that restores every byte. If nothing shrinks it, it comes back

as-is — never larger.

  • TRUSTREAM: telemetry/log compression on PCC's meter.
  • Chamber, Agent-Rider, Warrant, CuNi: as described on their product pages.
  • Free tiers exist so you can try before paying. Paid tiers are described on the

pricing page; the pricing page wins if this doc disagrees.

3. Your responsibilities

  • Don't use the service for anything illegal, and don't upload anything you

don't have the right to process (malware, stolen data, other people's private

files).

  • Don't abuse the service: no hammering the API past the published rate limits,

no reselling access without asking us first.

  • Keep your API keys and credentials private. You're responsible for what happens

under your account until you tell us it's compromised.

4. What we promise (and don't)

  • We aim for the service to be up and working, but we make no uptime guarantee

until we publish an SLA. [PROPOSED — Corey decides whether to offer one.]

  • Everything is provided as-is. We don't warrant that compression will hit

any particular ratio on your data.

  • Our liability to you is capped at **what you paid us in the 12 months before

the claim**. [PROPOSED — standard, but Corey decides.]

  • We are not liable for your data beyond that cap — keep your own backups of

anything irreplaceable. (Our data-handling policy covers what we do with files

you send us.)

5. Paying, pausing, leaving

  • Subscriptions bill monthly or yearly in advance; usage bills in arrears.
  • Cancel anytime; service runs to the end of the paid period. What happens to

your stored data after that is in the data-handling policy (30-day grace, then

purge — proposed).

  • We can suspend accounts for abuse or non-payment, and we'll tell you why.

6. Changes

If we change these terms in a way that matters, we'll post the new version on

the site at least 14 days before it takes effect and note what changed.

Continued use after that means you accept them.

7. The boring-but-required bits

  • Governing law: New Jersey, USA. [PROPOSED — Corey decides.]
