How it works

1

Publish by hash

Every blob is stored under its SHA-256. Same bytes, same address — deduplication falls out for free, and tampering fails loudly.

2

Teams stay separate

Invite-only namespaces with per-team quotas. Your team's depot is yours alone — isolation is enforced, not promised.

3

Audit everything

Every publish and eviction lands in the ops log with seat, team, and size. Quota hits are refused with the reason stated.

What it is / what it isn't

What it is: The lab's memory for artifacts — snapshots, books, brokers, and builds your team needs to find again.

What it isn't: Not git hosting, not a CDN, not public storage. Invite-only, team-scoped, receipted.
