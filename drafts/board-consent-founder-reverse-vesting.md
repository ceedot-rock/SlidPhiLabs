# DRAFT — REQUIRES ATTORNEY REVIEW BEFORE SIGNING

## Action by Unanimous Written Consent of the Board of Directors
### Slid Phi Labs, Inc.

The undersigned, being all of the members of the Board of Directors of Slid Phi Labs, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "Company"), acting pursuant to Section 141(f) of the Delaware General Corporation Law, hereby adopt the following resolutions with the same force and effect as if duly adopted at a meeting:

### Recitals

WHEREAS, on November 3, 2026, the Company issued 6,720,000 shares of Common Stock to Corey Ptaszenski (Certificate CS-1) and 1,280,000 shares of Common Stock to Robert Ptaszenski (Certificate CS-2) (collectively, the "Founder Shares"); and

WHEREAS, it was the intent of the Company and each founder that the Founder Shares vest over time, but no vesting schedule was recorded at issuance; and

WHEREAS, each founder has timely filed (or will timely file) an election under Section 83(b) of the Internal Revenue Code with respect to the Founder Shares;

### Resolved

1. **Vesting schedule imposed.** Each founder's Founder Shares shall henceforth vest as follows (the "Vesting Schedule"), with a vesting commencement date of November 3, 2026:
   - **Upfront tranche.** 20% of each founder's Founder Shares vest immediately upon execution of the Restricted Stock Agreements: 1,344,000 shares to Corey Ptaszenski and 256,000 shares to Robert Ptaszenski.
   - **Cliff.** No further shares vest before November 3, 2027 (the "Cliff"). On the Cliff date, an additional 20% of each founder's total Founder Shares vest (1,344,000 to Corey, 256,000 to Robert), for a cumulative 40% vested.
   - **Monthly installments.** The remaining 60% vest in 36 substantially equal monthly installments beginning December 3, 2027, with full vesting on November 3, 2030: 112,000 shares per month to Corey Ptaszenski (36 × 112,000 = 4,032,000), and 21,333 shares per month to Robert Ptaszenski for 35 months with a final installment of 21,345 shares (35 × 21,333 + 21,345 = 768,000).
   - All vesting beyond the upfront tranche is subject to the founder's continuous service to the Company through each vesting date.

2. **Repurchase right.** All unvested Founder Shares as of any service termination shall be subject to repurchase by the Company at the original purchase price per share ($0.00001), on terms set forth in the Restricted Stock Agreements below.

3. **Restricted Stock Agreements.** The officers of the Company are authorized to prepare and enter into a Restricted Stock Purchase Agreement (reverse vesting) with each founder, memorializing the Vesting Schedule (including the upfront tranche, Cliff, monthly installments, and rounding) and the Company's repurchase right, substantially in the form reviewed by Company counsel.

4. **Carta records.** Following execution of both Restricted Stock Agreements, the officers are authorized to update the Company's Carta records (Certificates CS-1 and CS-2) to reflect the Vesting Schedule.

5. **General authority.** The officers are authorized to take all actions necessary or advisable to carry out the intent of these resolutions, including engaging counsel and a CPA to confirm tax treatment.
