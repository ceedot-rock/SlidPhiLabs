What PCC is

PCC is hosted compression for your files. You send a file; you get back the smallest faithful copy — and when you restore it, every single byte is identical to the original. A smaller file, guaranteed identical when unzipped.

Here is why that matters. Storage and bandwidth cost real money, and most files carry far more redundancy than they need to. PCC runs your file through a tournament of compression methods and keeps whichever one wins — then proves the win by decoding it and checking every byte. In one live receipt, a 51 MB file came back as 16.7 MB, every byte verified on restore.

Lossless is a promise, not a hope. If nothing can shrink your file, PCC stores it as-is — it never makes a file bigger. And the proof is public: on the standard Silesia benchmark set, PCC packed the corpus to 51,498,645 bytes, with machine receipts you can inspect.

It is hosted, so our machines do the work — you use it from the website, over HTTP, from npm, or through MCP. The first 2 GB each month are free; after that it is 8¢ per GB, with a $39-a-month Pro plan (200 GB included), a $390 year plan, and a day pass for short runs.

Live log and telemetry compression rides on the same plan: TRUSTREAM packs your streams in small tiles while PCC handles your files. One meter, both doors.
