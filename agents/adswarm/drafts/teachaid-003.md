I built a study habit that finally stuck: one textbook chapter per night with an AI teacher that refuses to move on until I actually pass.

The setup that worked for me: upload the book (or use one of the 43 built-ins), read the chapter, then do the readiness chat. The teacher cites the chapter for every claim, and says "not in this chapter" instead of guessing — which honestly taught me how often I was fooling myself about knowing things.

What keeps me honest is that passing isn't a vibe. Score 70+ with at most two review items, or the chapter stays locked. Final chapter at 91+ earns the mastery certificate. My progress lives on my device, and the whole core loop is free.

Curious how others handle the "I read it but didn't learn it" problem — what actually forces the knowledge check for you?
